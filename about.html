At Olive & Oak, guests are welcomed into an atmosphere where sophistication meets warmth. Soft lighting, natural wood accents, and the gentle aroma of fresh herbs create an immersive dining experience inspired by the timeless charm of Italy.

Our culinary philosophy is rooted in authenticity, emphasizing ingredients sourced from regional Italian farmers and artisans. Every dish is crafted with precision—honoring classic flavors while embracing refined, contemporary techniques.

From handmade pastas to delicately prepared seafood, each plate showcases the artistry of our chefs. The menu shifts with the seasons, ensuring that every visit offers fresh inspirations and beautifully curated flavor combinations.

Complementing the cuisine is our sommelier-guided wine selection, featuring vintages from Italy’s most celebrated vineyards. Paired with attentive service and a serene ambiance, Olive & Oak promises a dining experience that delights the senses and lingers in memory.
